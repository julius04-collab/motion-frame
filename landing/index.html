From link to film in three steps

Figma and screenshot tools give you a still picture. motionframe opens your site in a real browser and records it moving.

  1. 1

    Paste your link

    We record your site scrolling top to bottom, once for desktop and once for phone. You watch it record in the mockup.

  2. 2

    Style it

    Pick a device, backdrop, format, length and music. Every change shows straight away, without recording again.

  3. 3

    Download the MP4

    Your browser makes the video file. Post it on X, LinkedIn or Instagram, or drop it into a client deck.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, while motionframe is in beta. Films carry a small “made with motionframe” badge in the corner. A Pro plan will remove it.

What will Pro include?

No badge, priority recording and early access to new devices and features. Join the list to get a founding-member price when it opens.

Which sites work?

Any public website, including Framer, Webflow, Wix and Vercel sites. Pages behind a login, or on your own computer, can’t be recorded. Only film sites you own or have permission to show.

How long does it take?

Recording takes about a minute for a typical page. Downloading takes about as long as the film itself on a laptop. Phones can export too, but they’re slower on long films.

Do you keep my videos?

No. Recordings are deleted from our server after two hours, and the final film is made in your own browser.
