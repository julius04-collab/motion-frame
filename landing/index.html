Questions

Which sites work?

Any public website, including Framer, Webflow, Wix and Vercel sites. Pages behind a login, or running on your own computer, can’t be recorded. Only film sites you own or have permission to show.

How long does it take?

Recording takes about a minute for a typical page. Downloading takes about as long as the film itself on a laptop. Phones can download too, but they’re slower on long films.

Can I choose how long the video is?

Yes. Let motionframe fit the length to your page, or pick 10, 15, 20, 30 or 45 seconds. Music always fits the length you choose.

Can I use my own music?

Yes. Upload a song and it loops smoothly to fit the film. Only use music you have the rights to, because social apps may mute copyrighted songs.

Do you keep my videos?

No. Recordings are deleted from our server after two hours, and the final film is made in your own browser.
